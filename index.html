Sohyun Kim

M.S. Student in Mobility · KAIST

My research focuses on probabilistic state estimation and nonlinear filtering, with applications to missile guidance and railway vehicle condition monitoring. My current research interests also include brain-inspired computational models for spatial state estimation.

I also work on Bayesian inference for threat assessment in manned-unmanned teaming (MUM-T).
